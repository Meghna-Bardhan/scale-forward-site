👋 Introduction

Scale Forward - The Scalable Engineering Conference 2026 is our first internal technology event where engineers from all alliances will share their knowledge, showcase projects, and inspire innovation across the company.

Scale Forward is the Scalable Capital engineering conference for 2026, hosted by Scalable Capital in Berlin, built for Scalable engineers across alliances.

🗓️ Key Dates: March 11th, 2026
📍 Venue: Alte Münze in Berlin
🧭 Theme: “Building the Future of Scalable Engineering”
👥 Audience: All Scalable engineers
